Updating the numbers

  1. Export the new Primary ATS reports from Salesforce as Excel files.
  2. In the project folder run python3 scripts/build_inventory.py ~/Downloads/"Primary ATS"*.xlsx
  3. Commit and push. Render publishes the update on its own within a minute or two.
