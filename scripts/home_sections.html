    The developer/G Developments

    Fifty years of building whole towns, not compounds.

    G Developments began as NEWGIZA Real Estate and Development, founded in 2006 by Mahmoud El-Gamal with Salah Diab. Its towns keep the built footprint to roughly a tenth of the land and give the rest to parks, golf, lagoons and schools, so the value holds as the neighbourhood matures.
