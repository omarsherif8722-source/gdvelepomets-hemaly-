G Developments/NEWKAIRO

One city, four ways to live in it.

NEWKAIRO sits on the Cairo–Suez Road in New Heliopolis, opposite Madinaty. Only 13% of the land is built; the rest is golf, parks, schools and The Boulevard. Choose a parcel to step inside.

Everything inside the gates.

NEWKAIRO brings the NEWGIZA model to East Cairo: a whole town around a championship golf course, with schools, sports, work and hospitality within walking distance or a tram ride.

  • Golf An 18-hole championship course with a golf hotel, clubhouse and lake.
  • Schools Three international schools and a university campus.
  • The Boulevard Downtown retail, dining and offices along an electric tram line.
  • Sports and health Two sports clubs and a medical hub.
