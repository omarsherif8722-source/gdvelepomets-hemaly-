## 1. Executive Summary

**G Developments** (historically established as **NEWGIZA Real Estate & Development**) is one of Egypt's premier luxury real estate master-developers. Founded in **2006** by veteran Egyptian businessman and visionary **Mahmoud El-Gamal** (alongside co-founder Salah Diab) and anchored by over **45–50 years of cumulative market experience**, the company pioneered Egypt's shift from isolated residential compounds to fully integrated, self-sustaining destination cities.

Originating with its iconic **1,550+ acre NEWGIZA** flagship city overlooking West Cairo's Giza plateau, the corporate platform unified under the **G Developments** banner to scale its low-density urban philosophy across four high-demand Egyptian corridors:
- **East Cairo:** **NEWKAIRO** (including *krypton*, *Neon Views*, *Golf Collection — Argon & Cerium Views*, and *Urban Living*)
- **West Cairo:** **NEWGIZA** (*Cypher by Autograph Collection · Marriott*, *Twilight Residence*, *Meditown*, *House of Giza*), **IVY New Zayed**, and **City View**
- **North Coast (Sahel):** **Seashell Sidi Abdelrahman**, **Seashell Ras El Hekma** (*Waterfront*, *Lagoon Views*, *G Residence*), **Playa Ghazala** (*G Villas RTM*, *G Village*), **Playa Ras El Hekma** (*Phase 3 G Serviced Chalets*), and **Hacienda Red**
- **Red Sea (Ain Sokhna):** **Ein Bay** (*The G Einbay Golf Resort*) and **Ein Resort**

## 2. Leadership & Founding Heritage

### Mahmoud El-Gamal — Founder & Chairman, G Developments · CEO, G Hotels
With more than five decades shaping landmark Egyptian real estate, tourism, and urban infrastructure, Mahmoud El-Gamal is recognized as one of the country's foremost community builders. His development ethos centers on architectural permanence, ultra-low building footprints (typically **9%–13%** built-up area, reserving **87%–90%** for landscapes, golf fairways, and swimmable water), and institutional anchors—universities, British K–12 schools, tertiary healthcare hubs, championship golf, and five-star hospitality—that protect homeowner quality of life and long-term capital appreciation.

### Corporate Vision & Mission
- **Vision:** To establish Egypt's premier integrated living and coastal destinations across West Cairo, East Cairo, the North Coast, and Ain Sokhna—communities that elevate daily life, knowledge, leisure, and enduring asset value.
- **Mission:** Master-plan human-scale, low-density ecosystems rooted in the **Live · Learn · Work · Play** philosophy, supported by globally respected architecture firms (such as *Alchemy Architecture*, *Die Stadt*, *Gensler* collaborators, and *NEWGIZA Design Studio*), international hospitality operators (*Autograph Collection by Marriott* & *G Hotels*), and disciplined community management.

## 4. The Four Core Development Pillars

1. **The NEWGIZA Blueprint:** Multi-decade proven execution—active 18-hole golf course, accredited **New Giza University (NGU)**, **New Giza Sporting Club (NGSC)**, international K–12 campuses, and **New Giza Health Park**.
2. **Ultra-Low Footprint Master-Planning:** Dedicating **87%–90%** of land to open greenery, elevated fairways, swimmable crystal lagoons, reflection lakes, and pedestrian corridors.
3. **Branded Hospitality & Managed Yields:** In-house **G Hotels** hospitality management (Phase 3 *G Serviced Chalets* at Playa Ras El Hekma) alongside global alliances including **Autograph Collection by Marriott** (*Cypher* at NEWGIZA) and serviced urban living (*krypton* at NEWKAIRO).
4. **Institutional Value Drivers:** Anchoring communities with premier education (**The King's School Canterbury Cairo** at IVY, **NGU** at NEWGIZA, 3 international schools + university planned at NEWKAIRO) and medical hubs (**Meditown**).
